Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here is a clear breakdown of how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address, phone number, and billing details. We also collect the audio recordings and transcripts of calls made via our AI agents.

2. Use of Data

We use the collected data to provide, maintain, and improve our services. Call transcripts are processed by third-party LLM providers (e.g., Groq) solely for the purpose of executing the AI voice agent logic.

3. Data Protection

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information. Call recordings are stored securely and can only be accessed by the account owner and authorized administrators.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@voxify.ai.
